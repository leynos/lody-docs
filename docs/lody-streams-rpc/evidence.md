Every citation of the form `E-…` in this documentation resolves to a row below.
Lody sources are linked at commit
[`61bd9701a`](https://github.com/LodyAI/Lody/tree/61bd9701a5ed6899a9f42787df682acfc3fd7d4b);
line numbers refer to that commit. Upstream sources are files inside the
published packages, linked through unpkg or crates.io, with line numbers from
the package as published.

The **Basis** column says how each row was established:

- **Checked**: reported by a research pass, then verified mechanically: the
  cited lines of the pinned source contain the decisive identifier or
  literal for the row.
- **Reported**: reported by a research pass and not checked mechanically.
  This covers whole-document citations, which have no line range, and
  sources that were not available locally. Treat any line numbers as
  approximate.

The basis of a row is about the citation, not about the claim. Whether a
claim is implemented, tested, intended, inferred or unknown is stated where
the claim is made.
